## Terrain

```text
Create a replacement terrain texture atlas for a side-view mining game. Use the attached old four tiles only as MATERIAL IDENTITY reference, not as the rendering style to preserve. The old assets have unwanted dark square outlines, shiny pebbles, and repetitive diagonal noise. Redesign all the geology coherently.
OUTPUT: exactly 3 equal columns by 2 equal rows, no gutters, no outer margin, no labels. Each cell is a complete seamless repeating texture with a native 32x32 logical pixel grid. Top row LEFT TO RIGHT: warm muted brown DIRT, neutral cool gray ROCK, muted sandy ochre SAND. Bottom row LEFT TO RIGHT: desaturated pale blue ICE, dark charcoal mostly-cooled MAGMA with only tiny dull ember fissures, GRASS-TOP DIRT. Grass-top is the SAME dirt as top-left with an irregular 3-pixel-high muted moss grass cap at the very top.
CRITICAL: every regular cell fills all four edges with the material and wraps seamlessly horizontally AND vertically when tiled with itself. NO square outline, bevel, frame, border strip, edge darkening, tile perimeter shadow, centered mound, individual brick, or regular checkerboard. Broad flat irregular clusters should continue THROUGH the cell edges. No visible cell perimeter within a material. Grass cell only wraps horizontally.
STYLE: hand-authored restrained pixel art, flat low-relief earth cross-section, 4-5 closely spaced shades per material, hard pixels but soft overall contrast. Large calm midtone fields with a few small irregular rough inclusions, balanced nondirectional pattern. Dirt looks like packed earth not orange cobblestones. Rock is solid bedrock with subtle irregular patches, NOT rounded separate rocks. Sand has sparse short strata, not speckled glitter. Ice has subdued irregular frost facets, not bright white diagonal crack grid. Magma is overwhelmingly cold dark stone with rare subdued small ember gaps, no glow. Avoid shiny highlights, black outlines, high-frequency noise, smooth gradients, photorealism, glossy rounded nuggets, directional diagonal striping. All six cells must feel like one art set. Pixel size uniform across the six textures. Entire sheet opaque.
```

## Underground

```text
Create one large seamless underground cavern BACKGROUND texture for a side-view pixel-art digging game. Attached image is the existing small reference for colors and mood ONLY. Expand the DESIGN to cover a much wider, more varied area rather than enlarging the same rocks. Flat orthographic rear wall cross-section, absolutely no perspective floor, no horizon, no lit cave opening, no foreground silhouettes. Entire image filled with subdued warm dark gray-brown compacted earth and bedrock, matching reference muted brown-gray palette. This is background behind brighter mineable blocks.
Target native logical canvas 512x512 pixels, with consistent crisp tiny pixel clusters; detail scale is deliberately small across this large canvas. Show MANY locally different regions of subtle uneven strata, irregular rough embedded rock patches, a few thin discontinuous dark crevices, gentle scattered textural breaks. Large natural areas of calmer midtone vary across the canvas. No identifiable repeated motif, no small repeating wallpaper blocks, no large boulders filling the frame. Broad variations should span tens to hundreds of pixels while individual marks have the same small pixel scale as 32px game blocks.
Seamless repeat left/right AND top/bottom. Fill every edge naturally, no perimeter line, frame, edge shading, border stripe, vignette, atlas grid, gutters, labels, text. Crucial: edge values must continue without a straight line or large contrast break. Restrained 5-7 stepped shades, low contrast, no glossy pebble shading, no antialiasing, no blur, no photographic noise, no ores, crystals, lava, props or lighting effects. Single opaque square texture, not a sprite sheet, no sample tiling preview.
```
